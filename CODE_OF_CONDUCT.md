# Code of Conduct

Hermes Tavern is a collaborative open-source project. All contributors and
participants in issues, pull requests, and reviews are expected to behave in a
professional and respectful way.

This repo follows these principles:

- Be respectful and courteous in all communication.
- Keep discussions inclusive, constructive, and focused on project quality.
- Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against others.
- Do not engage in trolling, doxxing, privacy violations, or personal attacks.
- Reject offensive or abusive language.

When reporting problems or concerns:

- Keep descriptions factual and focused on repository content and behavior.
- Do not post credentials, API keys, tokens, passwords, or other sensitive account
  details in public contribution channels.
- Redact anything that could identify private data.

A project maintainer may remove, edit, or close contributions that violate this
Code of Conduct.
